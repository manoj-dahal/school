The school

A public institution in Shantinagar

Shree Siddeshwor Secondary School is a public school in Shantinagar, New Baneshwor, Kathmandu Metropolitan City, Bagmati Province. Education is offered from Early Childhood Development (ECD) to Grade 10.

As per the 2081 IEMIS report of the Center for Education and Human Resource Development, the school has 506 enrolled students. It operates as a co-educational day school, with an ERO score of 72.58.
